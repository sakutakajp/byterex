# Introducing Byterex

This is a sample post for checking how the blog displays content.

Byterex is **a project that aims to automate household chores with AI and robotics**. Our first goal is to build a robot that recognizes, picks up, and sorts laundry.

## Our First Goal

We break the task of putting laundry away into three steps:

1. Recognize laundry using a camera
2. Pick it up with a robotic arm
3. Sort it by type

## System Overview

Built on LeKiwi / LeRobot, the system will combine a robotic arm, cameras, a Raspberry Pi, and an AI accelerator.

| Hardware | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Robotic arm | Pick up and move laundry |
| Camera | Capture laundry and its surroundings |
| Raspberry Pi and AI accelerator | Handle recognition and control |

## What We Will Share

This blog will document the development process, including hardware preparation, assembly, and experiments with recognition and manipulation.
